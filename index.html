why miyajima?

The floating torii, the tide that comes and goes, and deer on the beach. This site is my small version of the island.

can I feed the deer?

Yes. Tap the sand at the bottom of the screen to drop a shika senbei (deer cracker). Tap a deer and it bows back.

where’s the music from?

Your browser plays it live: a koto-like pluck wandering through a Japanese scale, with waves underneath. Hit the speaker in the top corner. Night mode plays it slower.

what are you working on?

More games and websites, plus a light novel I’m writing.

favourite player?

Stephen Curry. Range for days.

what’s the cake up top?

A momiji manju, the maple-leaf cake Miyajima is famous for. Tap it.
